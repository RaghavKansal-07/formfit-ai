How to film
  1. Film from the side Camera at 90° to your movement, not front or back.
  2. Keep your whole body in frame Head to feet visible for the entire set.
  3. Camera at hip height About 2–3 m away, phone held still.
  4. Good lighting Plain background, no strong backlight.
